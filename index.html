GiGi Hermes

A private automation used by one person to read the Gmail and Calendar of its own Google account. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Privacy policy

GiGi Hermes reads Gmail messages and Calendar events only from the single Google account that authorized it, to write summaries for that account's owner. The data is processed on the owner's own computer and by the AI model provider the owner uses. It is not sold and not shared with anyone else. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Terms of service

This app is for its owner's personal use only and is provided as is, without warranty. No one else may use it.
